PrintHive

Purdue senior design · 3D printing workflows

Contributed to student and operator workflows for a 3D print queue at Purdue’s Hive makerspace. The team prototype covers STL upload, 3D preview, queue tracking, and build plate clearance. Document Accessibility Checker

Designed and developed the frontend for uploading documents, connecting to the analysis service, and displaying findings. The team’s checker evaluates HTML, PDF, DOCX, and plain text against supported WCAG rules and suggests fixes. A later version also applies limited automatic repairs.
